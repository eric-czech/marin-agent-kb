# Writing

- Prioritize the reader’s purpose and the few points that matter most.
- When developing PRs or GitHub issues, assume no conversation context. Introduce concepts, scope, and terminology before using them.
- Explain why evidence matters through meaningful comparisons, rather than factual recitation.
- Support interpretations with evidence. Never manufacture insight, relevance, or practical value.
- Choose concrete examples that reveal the underlying logic.
- Use plain, precise, professional language. No slogans, invented jargon, or marketing style.
- Be concise without becoming cryptic. Preserve necessary definitions and constraints.
- Group related content. Put essentials first and supporting detail behind drilldowns.
- Make formatting reflect importance. Use emphasis sparingly and tables or visuals purposefully.
- Revise the whole piece for coherence. Remove repetition, obvious caveats, and edit history.
